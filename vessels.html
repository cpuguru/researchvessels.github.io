Status uses one vocabulary for ships and platforms: Most ships come from the legacy registry and stay Unconfirmed until a current source is checked; see how verification works.

Crewed includes minimally crewed ships. Uncrewed covers remotely operated, supervised autonomous and fully autonomous platforms. Records whose autonomy is unknown appear only when the toggle is All. Data: data/vessels.json and data/platforms.json. Where a homeport matches the World Port Index (Pub. 150), the WPI number and UN/LOCODE are shown under the name. Ports not in the WPI, including Lewes, Delaware, have no WPI number.
