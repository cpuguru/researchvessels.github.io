Reading this list

  • Click a column heading to sort; click it again to reverse. The address keeps your filters, so a filtered list can be shared.
  • Vessels are matched to the vessel directory by the exact operator or owner name; platforms are linked through each platform's owner, operator, developer, host or funder roles. Both counts include the institution's groups.
  • Institution IDs are not case-sensitive: institution.html?id=WHOI and ?id=whoi open the same page.
  • Machine-readable list: data/institutions.json (CC BY 4.0, “Data compiled by Doug White / researchvessels.org”).
