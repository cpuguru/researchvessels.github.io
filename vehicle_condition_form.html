Vehicle Condition Form draft

A checklist for inspecting an uncrewed vehicle (glider, AUV, ROV, USV, towed system or float) before a deployment. It follows the same idea as a ship condition form: a shared header, then checks, then sign-off.

Private to you. Everything you type stays in your browser. Nothing is saved, uploaded or sent unless you choose to print the page, download the file, or send an e-mail yourself. This draft is not an official inspection record or a substitute for your operator’s safety management system. Questions: ships@oceandata.org.
