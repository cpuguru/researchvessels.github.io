Who can update a vessel

  1. Claim the vessel with your work email at the operator or owning institution. We send a sign-in link to that address.
  2. We cross-check the operator against public fleet registries (UNOLS, EUROFLEETS/ERVO, POGO, IOC/IODE, national fleet lists).
  3. An editor approves the claim. Several people can hold one vessel (marine superintendent, technicians, read-only), and claims move to the new operator when a ship is sold.

Every change is reviewed before it is published, and each field on the public page shows its source, who changed it (by role), and when. Personal contact details are never published; use role mailboxes.

Uncrewed vehicles: Vehicle Condition Form draft

A printable pre-deployment checklist for gliders, AUVs, ROVs, USVs and floats: hull and pressure housing, battery chemistry and certification, communications, remote control and emergency abort, tracking and recovery aids, launch and recovery gear, sensor calibration dates, software and firmware, and insurance and permits (including foreign-waters consent).

Open the Vehicle Condition Form It runs in your browser, saves nothing and sends nothing. You print it, download it or e-mail it yourself.

Your data, back to you

Proposed API (read is public; write needs an operator key)

GET /api/v1/vessels?operator=…Your fleet (JSON, CSV, or GeoJSON)
GET /api/v1/vessels/{id}One vessel with dated owner, operator, homeport, and flag history
GET /api/v1/vessels/{id}/cruises?from=&to=Schedule (also as iCal)
GET /api/v1/vessels/{id}/track?from=&to=&res=10m|rawTrack points or lines (GeoJSON)
GET /api/v1/search/area?bbox=&from=&to=Ships and cruises in an area
POST /api/v1/changesSubmit a change set (reviewed before publishing)

Documented with OpenAPI. Bulk snapshots are published yearly with a DOI under CC BY 4.0 (proposed).
