Where were ships?

Draw an area and choose years to see which research ships crossed it on an imported cruise track, which cruises they ran, and where to look for the data.

Cruise tracks, not positions. Lines are imported cruise navigation: R2R (Rolling Deck to Repository, CC0) for U.S. academic ships and NCEI / NOAA Ocean Exploration for NOAA Ship Okeanos Explorer. Only ships listed in the track index have them; every other ship has no track data yet, and nothing is estimated or drawn from schedules.
