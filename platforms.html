Platforms beyond ships

Gliders, AUVs, ROVs, uncrewed surface and aerial vehicles, and other non-ship research platforms. This first set covers the University of Delaware Robotic Discovery Laboratories in Lewes, built only from public sources.

Draft – unverified; corrections welcome at ships@oceandata.org. Nothing here has been confirmed by the University of Delaware. Unknown values are left blank, and every spec shows where it came from. The status labels are our reading of the evidence, not an official statement.

Reading this list

  • Status is our reading of public evidence, dated on each page:
  • Two vehicles are called OTIS. The OTIS glider (ud_275) dates from 2012; the OTIS ROV was donated to UD in 2025. They are separate records.
  • Specs show their basis: unit metadata from a data service, a figure the operator published (often years old), a register entry, or a project report. Manufacturer brochure values are not used.
  • The ship records are in the vessel directory. Deployment launch vessels link there when the ship is already listed.
  • Data: data/platforms.json (CC BY 4.0, credit "Data compiled by Doug White / researchvessels.org" and the source shown on each record).
