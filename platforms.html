Platforms beyond ships

Gliders, AUVs, ROVs, crewed submersibles, uncrewed surface and aerial vehicles, and other non-ship research platforms, listed by the institution or group that owns or operates them. The first set covers the University of Delaware Robotic Discovery Laboratories; WHOI, Rutgers RU COOL, NOAA, public U.S. Navy entries and a sample of international operators were added as examples. Everything here is built only from public sources.

Draft – unverified; corrections welcome at ships@oceandata.org. No operator has confirmed these records. Unknown values are left blank, and every spec shows where it came from. The status labels are our reading of the evidence, not an official statement. Military entries carry only openly published information and are flagged as limited.

Reading this list

  • Status is our reading of public evidence, dated on each page:
  • Institutions and groups nest: a lab or facility sits inside its institution (for example RDL inside UD CEOE inside UD, or NDSF inside WHOI). Choosing an institution also shows its groups' platforms. Each platform can have several roles: owner, operator, developer, host facility or funder. Browse them all on the institutions page.
  • Grouped view lists each platform under every top-level institution it is tied to, so a Navy-owned, WHOI-operated vehicle such as Alvin appears under both.
  • Two vehicles are called OTIS. The OTIS glider (ud_275) dates from 2012; the OTIS ROV was donated to UD in 2025. They are separate records.
  • Specs show their basis: unit metadata from a data service, a figure the operator published (often class-level), a register entry, a project report, or a secondary copy of operator figures. Manufacturer brochure values are not used.
  • The ship records are in the vessel directory. A few deep-sea vehicles (Alvin, Jason, Sentry, DSL-120A) are also listed there; their platform pages link across.
  • Data: data/platforms.json and data/institutions.json (CC BY 4.0, credit "Data compiled by Doug White / researchvessels.org" and the source shown on each record).
