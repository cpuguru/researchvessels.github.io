Uncrewed platforms

Gliders, AUVs, ROVs, uncrewed surface and aerial vehicles, profiling floats and other vehicles that work with, or instead of, a crewed research ship. This page counts what is in the registry, links to the filtered views, and gathers the related institutions, support ships and Resources.

Draft – unverified; corrections welcome at ships@oceandata.org. Counts come from public sources and show what the registry holds, not how many vehicles exist. A vehicle record can stand for a fleet.
